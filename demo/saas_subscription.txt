SAAS SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This SaaS Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of February 15, 2025 ("Effective Date") by and between CloudMetrics Inc. ("Provider") and Apex Financial Group ("Customer").

1. SERVICE DESCRIPTION
1.1 Provider shall make available its cloud-based analytics platform ("Service") via the internet.
1.2 Service includes: real-time dashboards, data ingestion, report generation, and API access.
1.3 Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain 99.5% uptime.

2. SUBSCRIPTION TERM
2.1 Initial term: twelve (12) months from Effective Date.
2.2 This Agreement auto-renews for successive twelve (12) month periods unless Customer provides notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days prior to end of current term.
2.3 Early termination by Customer requires payment of all remaining fees for the current term.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT
3.1 Annual subscription fee: $48,000, payable in monthly installments of $4,000.
3.2 Payment due within fifteen (15) days of invoice.
3.3 Late payments accrue interest at 2.5% per month.
3.4 Fees are non-refundable. No credits for partial months.
3.5 Provider may increase fees by up to 10% upon renewal with 60 days notice.

4. DATA AND PRIVACY
4.1 Customer retains all rights to its data.
4.2 Provider may access Customer data solely to provide the Service.
4.3 Provider shall not sell or share Customer data with third parties.
4.4 Provider shall implement industry-standard security measures.
4.5 Upon termination, Provider shall make data available for export for thirty (30) days, then delete all data.

5. INDEMNIFICATION
5.1 Customer shall indemnify Provider against all claims arising from Customer's use of the Service.
5.2 Provider shall indemnify Customer against third-party IP infringement claims.

6. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
6.1 NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES.
6.2 Provider's total liability shall not exceed the fees paid by Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.
6.3 Provider is not liable for data loss, service interruptions, or security breaches beyond its reasonable control.

7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
7.1 Provider retains all rights in the Service and platform.
7.2 Customer grants Provider a limited license to use Customer data for Service improvement.
7.3 Customer shall not reverse-engineer, decompile, or copy the Service.

8. SERVICE LEVELS
8.1 Provider shall respond to critical issues within four (4) hours.
8.2 Provider shall respond to non-critical issues within one (1) business day.
8.3 Service credits available only if uptime falls below 99.0% in a calendar month.

9. GOVERNING LAW
9.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York.
9.2 Disputes shall be resolved through arbitration in New York City.
