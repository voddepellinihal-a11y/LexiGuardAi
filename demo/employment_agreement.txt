EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of April 1, 2025 ("Effective Date") by and between NexGen Labs Inc. ("Employer") and Sarah Chen ("Employee").

1. POSITION AND DUTIES
1.1 Employee is hired as Senior Software Engineer, reporting to the VP of Engineering.
1.2 Employee shall devote full working time and best efforts to the performance of duties.
1.3 Employee shall not engage in any other employment or business activity without prior written consent.

2. COMPENSATION
2.1 Base salary: $145,000 per year, payable bi-weekly.
2.2 Signing bonus: $15,000, payable within 30 days of start date.
2.3 Annual performance bonus up to 20% of base salary, at Employer's sole discretion.
2.4 Stock options: 10,000 shares vesting over 4 years with 1-year cliff.

3. BENEFITS
3.1 Employer shall provide health, dental, and vision insurance.
3.2 Four (4) weeks paid time off per year.
3.3 401(k) match up to 4% of salary.

4. TERM AND TERMINATION
4.1 This is an at-will employment agreement. Either party may terminate at any time with two (2) weeks written notice.
4.2 Employer may terminate immediately for cause, including but not limited to: misconduct, breach of policy, dishonesty, or poor performance as determined solely by Employer.
4.3 Upon termination without cause, Employee shall receive severance equal to two (2) weeks base pay per year of service, contingent on signing a release.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
5.1 Employee agrees that all inventions, discoveries, code, designs, and intellectual property created during employment ("Work Product") shall be the sole and exclusive property of Employer.
5.2 Employee assigns all rights in Work Product to Employer, including moral rights.
5.3 Employee shall not use any employer resources for personal projects.
5.4 Employee shall not retain copies of any Work Product after termination.

6. NON-COMPETE
6.1 For a period of eighteen (18) months following termination, Employee shall not: (a) work for any direct competitor of Employer; (b) solicit any employee or customer of Employer; (c) disclose any trade secrets.
6.2 "Direct competitor" includes any company offering AI-powered analytics platforms, as determined by Employer in its sole discretion.
6.3 Non-compete applies worldwide.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY
7.1 Employee shall maintain strict confidentiality of all proprietary information.
7.2 Confidentiality obligations survive termination indefinitely.
7.3 Employee acknowledges that breach may cause irreparable harm.

8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
8.1 All disputes shall be resolved through mandatory binding arbitration.
8.2 Arbitration shall take place in the state selected by Employer.
8.3 Employer shall be entitled to recover attorneys fees.

9. GOVERNING LAW
9.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
