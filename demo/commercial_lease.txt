COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Commercial Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into as of March 1, 2025 ("Effective Date") by and between Pacific Real Estate Holdings LLC ("Landlord") and Urban Bistro LLC ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES
1.1 Landlord leases to Tenant the commercial space located at 456 Market Street, Suite 200, San Francisco, CA 94105 ("Premises"), comprising approximately 2,500 square feet.

2. TERM
2.1 Initial term: three (3) years from Effective Date.
2.2 Two (2) successive renewal options of three (3) years each, exercisable by Tenant with twelve (12) months written notice.

3. RENT
3.1 Base rent: $8,500 per month for Year 1.
3.2 Annual escalation: 5% increase each year.
3.3 Rent is due on the first day of each month.
3.4 Late fee: $250 if rent not received by the 5th of the month.
3.5 Additional 1.5% monthly interest on overdue amounts.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
4.1 Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $25,500 (three months rent).
4.2 Deposit refundable within sixty (60) days of lease termination, less any deductions for damages.
4.3 Landlord may apply deposit to unpaid rent or damages without notice.

5. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
5.1 Landlord responsible for structural repairs, roof, and exterior walls.
5.2 Tenant responsible for all interior maintenance, equipment, and fixtures.
5.3 Tenant shall maintain the Premises in good condition.
5.4 Landlord may enter Premises upon 24 hours notice for inspections and repairs.

6. USE OF PREMISES
6.1 Premises shall be used solely for restaurant operations.
6.2 Tenant shall comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and building rules.
6.3 Tenant shall not operate outside normal business hours (7 AM - 11 PM) without written consent.

7. ALTERATIONS
7.1 Tenant shall not make structural alterations without Landlord's prior written consent.
7.2 All alterations become property of Landlord upon installation.
7.3 Tenant shall restore Premises to original condition upon termination.

8. INSURANCE
8.1 Tenant shall maintain commercial general liability insurance of $2,000,000 per occurrence.
8.2 Tenant shall maintain property insurance covering all personal property and improvements.
8.3 Landlord named as additional insured on Tenant's policy.

9. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES
9.1 Tenant in default if: (a) rent unpaid for five (5) days; (b) any other covenant breached and not cured within thirty (30) days.
9.2 Upon default, Landlord may: terminate this Lease, re-enter Premises, recover all damages, and pursue all remedies at law or in equity.
9.3 Tenant shall pay all costs of collection, including reasonable attorneys fees.

10. INDEMNIFICATION
10.1 Tenant shall indemnify Landlord against all claims arising from Tenant's use of the Premises.
10.2 Landlord shall indemnify Tenant against claims arising from Landlord's negligence.

11. GOVERNING LAW
11.1 This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the State of California.
